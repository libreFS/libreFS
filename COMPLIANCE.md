# AGPLv3 Compliance

libreFS is free and open-source software licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE) (AGPLv3).

The AGPLv3 requires that any application that uses libreFS and is made available over a network must also make its complete source code available under a compatible license. This includes SaaS applications and hosted services.

libreFS cannot make the determination as to whether your application's usage is in compliance with the AGPLv3 license requirements. You should rely on your own legal counsel or licensing specialists to audit and ensure your application is in compliance with the licenses of libreFS and all other open-source projects with which your application integrates or interacts.

AGPLv3 licensing is complex and nuanced. We strongly encourage using experts in licensing to make any such determinations rather than relying on informal advice.

If the AGPLv3 does not work for your use case, please open a discussion on [GitHub](https://github.com/libreFS/libreFS/discussions) — the community is happy to help clarify the requirements.
